Keyboard bindings

Extraction field guide

  1. Prepare. Pick a map and owned equipment, or use the free recovery kit. Read the risk summary and optional insurance cost. Deployed items leave your stash until your raid is settled.
  2. Move and aim. Move with W/A/S/D. Click the view for mouse aim. Touch players drag the view and use the movement buttons. Arrow Left/Arrow Right and Arrow Up/Arrow Down aim without a mouse. Space jumps. Escape releases the mouse. Use Settings to remap every game action.
  3. Search and carry. Approach a cache and press E or Interact. Keep still until searching finishes. Moving, taking damage or performing another action interrupts a search, revival or boarding; looking and squad pings do not. Only one such interaction can run at a time. Open Backpack with B to take, inspect, equip, use, drop or protect eligible items. Slots and weight both matter. Protected storage excludes weapons, ammunition and recovery artifacts. Each sector has distinct salvage and a recovery artifact; check the map briefing and accept its contract before deploying. Reserved contract items cannot be accidentally sold or recycled.
  4. Progress. Extraction, exploration, machines and contracts earn raid XP. Ranks unlock weapon assembly blueprints that still require credits, materials and a workbench. Existing shops and recovery kits remain available. Ordinary-mode classes, pets, weapon upgrades and diamonds grant no raid bonuses.
  5. Survive. Aim at exposed machine cores. Leave the warning rings before an attack lands. Listen for charging machines: a Scout chirps three times, a Prowler growls upward, a Bulwark knocks twice, a Skimmer whistles downward and the Warden gives three rising furnace blasts. Nearby charge sounds indicate direction; warning rings and captions remain available with sound off. Fire with click, Fire or F; hold for automatic rifles, SMGs and support guns, or press once per shot with sidearms, shotguns and scout rifles. Aim with the captured right mouse button, hold Shift Left or toggle Aim on screen. Owned optics and scout rifles magnify the view; aiming does not grant extra damage. Shotguns fire eight spreading pellets with distance falloff. Reload with R. Select weapons with 1/2 and use medicine with 3. Armor and ammunition must be owned. Light, field and heavy armor absorb body hits using their condition; heavier rigs trade movement speed for protection. Head and leg hits bypass armor. New deployments have 100 health. Bleeding needs a dressing or medkit; leg injuries need a splint or trauma pack. Medical supplies have a two-second reuse delay. Inspect supplies and choose them in Backpack; armor can be removed or changed after a second out of damage. Injured squadmates can be revived or carried using Backpack controls.
  6. Use utilities. Bring or find an Ashveil smoke canister, Scrapchatter noise lure or Shatter explosive. Aim before using Utility / G, which throws the first carried utility; select a specific item with Throw toward aim in Backpack. Smoke hides enemies and disrupts machine sight, but does not stop bullets. Lures attract investigating machines. Charges have a delayed blast that can hurt you; keep clear of the warning ring.
  7. Coordinate. Open Squad / voice for markers, supply requests and squad status. Voice starts off; enable it before holding your push-to-talk key. Nearby voice includes opted-in rivals. Mute, block and report controls are in that panel.
  8. Extract. Map markers labeled E show exit sites. Read their schedules and access rules in Backpack. Called exits attract attention and have two three-seat departures. The timed lift has six seats. Maintenance hatches cost one access key per opening and take one person; the emergency winch costs two carried fuel and takes two people once. Costs are consumed when an exit opens, even if you cannot board. Stand in an open exit, Interact and complete boarding. Movement, damage, another action or leaving the area interrupts boarding. A carried teammate needs a second seat. Seat reservations are released if boarding is interrupted. Full or exhausted exits require another route. Your extraction does not end anyone else's raid.
  9. Return and recover. Review returned and lost items, insurance, costs and rewards in Raid records. Resolve protected overflow before deploying again. Death, timeout or disconnect can lose unsecured items; Back to base leaves your operator vulnerable in the raid. Reconnect within 45 seconds to continue.

Read current health, time and nearby interaction with H. Critical warnings are shown in text; item rarity is written out. Reduced motion removes optional sway, recoil motion, weather animation and pulsing indicators. The game still requires navigation and aiming; this guide does not claim complete assistive-technology support.
